# Home landing

## Scope and mode

- Target: `src/App.jsx`, related `src/styles.css` and `index.html`.
- Mode: Persuade.
- One-page landing for «Дары Синергии».

## Audience, job, action and proof

- Audience: sports clubs, veterinary organizations, sanatoria, private/family users and agricultural enterprises.
- Job: understand what the company produces, recognize a relevant application context and initiate direct contact.
- Action: universal «Получить консультацию» CTA leading to the confirmed email and phone contact.
- Proof currently available: three EAEU declarations of conformity for ozone generators, ozonated cosmetic oils and hydrolats/herbal extracts. No photos, cases, prices, testimonials, detailed specs or quantified effects may be invented.

## Approved direction

- Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-a-focused-source.png`.
- Hero headline: «Озон — сила природы.»
- Visual meaning correction: the hero field is a real full-bleed H.264 video of bubbles moving through dark water, with a generated in-project poster as its static fallback.
- Premium restraint: navigation and calls to action are text links, not filled square buttons.
- Page structure: source → three product directions → audience contexts → production and on-site installation → scientific restraint → contact.
- Memorable moment: a narrow stream of ozone bubbles enters a dark body of water and opens into a luminous, controlled field as the headline remains anchored in a large quiet zone.

## Component and material grammar

- Corners: 0–4px for controls and content; no pill language or rounded-card collection.
- Lines: 1px cyan/navy dividers at low opacity.
- Elevation: no ordinary shadows; depth comes from tonal navy fields, water refraction, particles and clipped media.
- Type: Manrope Variable geometric grotesk, tight display leading; IBM Plex Mono for micro labels.
- Motion: one orchestrated water/ozone field, slow parallax media, blur-reveal for section acts, mask/underline transitions for links and desktop Lenis snapping between authored narrative screens. Context panels snap to five exact stage positions; mobile and reduced-motion stay linear.

## Responsive and accessibility commitments

- Desktop: 12 columns; tablet/mobile: 6 columns; 20px margins and gutters where space permits.
- Widescreen: production and evidence compositions scale from viewport height and keep their complete content inside one snapped screen down to 800px viewport height.
- Mobile crops the water field around the ozone source; hero headline stays 56–64px minimum.
- Fullscreen navigation uses text links and staggered reveal.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` renders the static hero poster, removes video playback/parallax/section holds and keeps all content visible.
- Canvas and video are decorative; semantic text fully communicates the offer.
