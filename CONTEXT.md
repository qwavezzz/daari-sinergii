# Domain Context

## Product vocabulary

- **Системы озонации** — оборудование и решения, связанные с созданием и применением озона. Exact product configurations and specifications are not yet supplied.
- **Озонированные масла** — one of the company's product directions. Composition, indications, package sizes, and claims remain unspecified.
- **Гидролаты** — one of the company's product directions. Plant sources, volumes, and applications remain unspecified.
- **Отраслевое решение** — a path from an audience context to the relevant company direction; it is not a promise of a measured result.
- **Организации** — the primary website audience: sports and recovery centers, sanatoriums, SPA and pools, water treatment, veterinary and livestock operations, and agriculture.
- **Частные покупатели** — the secondary audience.
- **Вариант** — one complete presentation concept applied across every route while sharing the same factual content.
- **Демонстрационная заявка** — a locally validated form state that does not transmit or store personal data.

## Language constraints

- Use concise, professional Russian.
- Do not call unknown product details facts.
- Do not use medical promises, invented certificates, prices, metrics, testimonials, customer logos, or specifications.
- Use `Уточняется` or `PLACEHOLDER — уточнить` for missing factual content.
